OUR STORY
BIO
SELEXIONES is an underground LA radio station, broadcasting from Lincoln Heights. Two channels, one signal — UNO for what’s never been heard and DOS for what never fails. Built by Jesus German and 3EAS, two brothers rooted in the Los Angeles Chicano Underground.
Your support keeps the signal on — covering streaming infrastructure, licensing, and giving DJs a real platform to be heard. Every contribution goes straight back into keeping SELEXIONES independent and growing.
